1a

One next thing

Home answers one question: what do I do right now. Everything else on today's Home — daily bonus, practice-these, progress bar, trophy case, badge row — collapses into a single plan card and one progress line. Trophies and badges move to their own screen behind a link, so they stay a destination instead of wallpaper. The quiz header drops to one line; the score only appears once an answer is checked.

1b

Two lanes

Two kids of different ages share one install, so Home starts with who is playing. Picking a racer sets the level range, the star count, the practice-these list and the gym sets — no shared pile of 20 levels and 16 trophies to scroll past. Rewards become one goal per kid ("2 ★ to Track Champion") instead of a 16-tile locked grid, and the grown-up view is the only place both lanes appear together.

1c

Training ground (tablet-first)

The 500px column is the reason the app feels empty on a tablet. Here the bottom tab bar becomes a left rail, and the content splits: play on the left, status on the right. On a phone the rail collapses back to the current bottom nav and the right column stacks underneath, so it stays one layout rather than two apps.
